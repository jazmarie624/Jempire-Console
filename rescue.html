The four rules that make it work

1. Written agreement before the keysHer $650 is rent money on loan. “Paid in full by August 20, 2026,” in writing, notarized, and the car stays in your possession until it lands. Everything in this plan after Aug 20 assumes that money shows up.
2. Call Summer Cove Monday and give them Aug 21 in writingYou’re paying eleven days past a live notice. That call is the only thing standing between you and a filing. Call again on the 18th to reconfirm.
3. Flex before the bank on Sep 5The overdraft has no court date behind it. Rent does. If both can’t happen, Flex wins and the overdraft waits until Sep 18. Getting this backwards costs you $862.
4. Living money is untouchable in both directions$150 a week comes out first, every week. And it never gets raided for rent. If a week runs short, the dash target absorbs it — not the groceries.

A payment is a math problem a lender can work around with the right down payment. A repo is a door that closes.
The refinance endgame is genuinely excellentIf she buys it next year and refinances into her own name, the loan leaves your credit report completely. Your debt-to-income clears. Why you should let it go

It earns nothing nowMAS Host dropped the vehicle. The entire financial case for this car was that it generated income. That case is gone. You’d be paying $730 a month for an asset producing $0.
“Taking over payments” is not a legal thingThe loan stays in your name no matter what she promises. If she pays five months and stops, it’s your credit, your debt, your repo — a second one — and she drives away. Only a refinance into her name or an outright sale changes that.
It counts against you even while she’s payingLenders count the full $580 against your debt-to-income whether or not someone else sends the money. So until she refinances, the car you’re protecting for the SUV is also the thing standing in front of it.

If it breaks

Things will go sideways. That's not failure — it's Tuesday. Here's what to do instead of reaching for an app.

Timberline refuses partial paymentsDon't pay anything Monday. Hold all of it and pay the full $2,049.14 on Wednesday Aug 12 when child support lands. Two days late on a notice, paid in full, with a documented phone call — landlords almost never file on that. Call them Monday to tell them the date so it's on record.
A child support deposit doesn't landDo not borrow to cover it. Push a bill instead, in this order: car storage → Amscot → credit card minimums → light bill. Utilities and the car loan hold. Then add one dash shift to the next window.
The dash target comes up shortTake it out of the September cushion window, not out of rent or living money. Being $100 short in August is survivable; being $100 short on Sept 5 breaks Flex.
A kid emergency hitsUse the living money for the week and eat simple. That's what it's there for. If it's over $150 — that is a genuine emergency and the overdraft exists. One draw, written on the calendar, repaid next payday. Never an advance.
You miss the Flex Sept 5 paymentCall Flex the same day, before they draft. Ask what a late payment does to the account and whether the date can move. Don't let it silently fail — Flex reporting a default is what would take October's option away.
You're tempted by an advanceOpen Door 3 in the fork tab and read the Aug 21 line. That's not a lecture, it's arithmetic. Then check whether the thing you're covering can wait 48 hours for the next deposit. It almost always can.
The one rule underneath all of it: living money and rent money don't borrow from each other. Everything else on this page is negotiable.
