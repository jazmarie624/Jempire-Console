Where everything lives

The Forms

Osceola posts almost none of these. The Florida Bar posts all of them, free.

The master source

Every form numbered 7.3xx lives in the Florida Small Claims Rules PDF — free from The Florida Bar and flcourts.gov. Download it once, keep it on your iPad, and you have every form you will ever need in this case.

Osceola own forms page has only one small claims item: the Small Claims Packet, which is for starting a case. Nothing for default, nothing for collection.

Form 7.351 — the format for your motion

Titled "Format for Defendant's Motion," but its committee note says it may be adapted for use by the plaintiff. The entire official form is: the caption, a title line, the sentence "files this motion to ___, and as grounds therefore, states as follows:", then a certificate of service.

Everything else is your own words. That is why your motion in the Win tab looks the way it does.

Form 7.340 — Final Judgment

Pre-printed: "It is adjudged that the plaintiff(s), ___, recover from the defendant(s), ___, the sum of $___ on principal, $___ as prejudgment interest, $___ for attorneys' fees, with costs of $___, all of which shall bear interest at the rate of ___% per year..."

You fill in: both names · principal $3,670.00 · costs $388.63 · the interest rate from the Florida CFO · his last known address · last four of his Social if known.

Never delete the Enforcement Paragraph. It is marked optional on the form, but Rule 7.221 says the judge shall include it when you request it. Without it, you get no Fact Information Sheet.

Form 7.343 — Fact Information Sheet

He fills it out under oath. It demands his employer name and address, rate of pay, average paycheck, other income, every checking and savings account with account numbers, real estate, every vehicle with VIN, any property transfer over $100 in the last year, and who owes him money.

He must attach: last pay stub · last 3 statements for every account · vehicle titles · deeds or leases · last 2 tax returns.

The form says in capitals: serve it on the judgment creditor, DO NOT FILE IT WITH THE COURT. It comes to your mailbox. Nobody else sees it. Nobody but you is counting the 45 days.

Form 7.342 — Hearing in Aid of Execution

You personally qualify and almost nobody uses it. Rule 7.221(b) limits it to a judgment creditor who is a natural person and was not represented by an attorney before judgment. That is you exactly.

It is ex parte — no hearing needed to ask. The judge orders him to appear at a set time, 45+ days after judgment, examined under oath about earnings, financial status, and assets, and to bring the completed Fact Information Sheet. Failure to comply is grounds for contempt.

Forms 7.344, 7.341, 7.347

7.344 Order to Show Cause — when he ignores the hearing. Requires a process server.

7.341 Execution — commands the sheriff to levy. Rule 7.200: issues on your oral request, no written motion.

7.347 Satisfaction of Judgment — file only when he has paid in full. Filing early wipes out your judgment permanently.

Garnishment — no form exists anywhere

Chapter 77 has no approved statewide form and Osceola posts none. You draft the motion; the clerk issues the writ. Ask the Civil Division at 407-742-3500 whether they keep an unposted local packet — many clerks do even when it is not on the website.
