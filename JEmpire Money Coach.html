💰 Save & spend like a queen
The habits that make $150/week actually hold — and the money moves that keep you out of the hole.

💵 How to pull your $150 a week

  • The envelope way: the day money lands, pull $150 cash. When the envelope's empty, you're done till next week. Hardest to overspend.
  • The 2nd-card way: keep a separate debit/prepaid card, move $150 to it weekly, and only spend from that card for groceries + gas.
  • The auto-transfer way: set a recurring $150 transfer (Navy Federal lets you schedule) into a "Weekly" account every payday. Spend only from there.
Pick ONE and stick to it. The method matters less than the wall between "weekly money" and "everything else."

✂️ Where to save (biggest wins first)

  • Gas: use a cashback gas app (see Apps tab), fill at Sam's/warehouse pumps, combine trips into one loop.
  • Groceries: plan the week's meals before you shop, buy store brands, lean on your Aldi + Sam's bulk system. A list = no impulse buys.
  • Subscriptions: audit every auto-charge once. Cancel anything you forgot you had — that's free money back every month.
  • Eating out: the tight-week rule — cook, don't order. $25 saved on takeout is $25 toward rent.

👑 Where your money should go (in order)

  1. Rent fund — before anything fun.
  2. Bills — in the order on the Rent Rescue tab.
  3. $150 weekly — your living.
  4. Savings — every leftover dollar. This is the buffer that ends the late-rent cycle for good.
The mindset shift: you're not "broke till payday" anymore — you're building a cushion. Every $20 you don't spend is a brick in the wall between you and another late notice.
